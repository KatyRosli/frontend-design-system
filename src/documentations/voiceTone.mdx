# Voice and Tone Principles

A product's interface is a conversation between the user and the system.

Every message, label, button, error state, and notification communicates something about the product.

The Frontend Design System uses a voice that is:

- Clear
- Helpful
- Confident
- Inclusive
- Human

Our goal is to help users understand what is happening and what they should do next.

# Clear

## Make information easy to understand

Use simple language and avoid unnecessary complexity.

Users should immediately understand:

- What happened
- Why it happened
- What action they can take

Avoid technical language unless it is necessary.
